# Literal English Translation of the Vulgate Book of Job

This repository contains a close, literal English translation of the Latin Vulgate text of the Book of Job (*Liber Iobi*), taken from the public-domain CLTK Latin Library corpus.

## Source
- Original Latin: https://github.com/cltk/lat_text_latin_library/blob/master/bible/job.txt
- Translation approach: word-for-word / close literal rendering that retains verse numbers and chapter structure as faithfully as natural English permits.

## Files
- `00_Introduction.md` – title and notes
- `01_Chapters.md` – Chapters 1–7
- `02_Chapters.md` – Chapters 8–14
- `03_Chapters.md` – Chapters 15–21
- `04_Chapters.md` – Chapters 22–28
- `05_Chapters.md` – Chapters 29–35
- `06_Chapters.md` – Chapters 36–42 (complete)

The entire Book of Job is now present in literal English translation.
